statement語錄

there is no such word as “success” in my dictionary. success means an ending, a full stop.
i prefer to keep investing myself, moving forward, letting every ending be a comma instead.我的字典中沒有「成功」兩字。成功，意味著終結，畫一個句號。
我還是喜歡一直投入，每一次都是逗號便好。

it is not enough to do what you imagine in your mind. you must also listen to what your heart is telling you.不單要做自己腦裡所想的，要聽自己心裡跟自己說的話。

i have always believed this:
life is full of choices, big and small. the meaning of life, and the wisdom it teaches us, lie in facing these choices,
making them responsibly, and taking responsibility for every decision we make by following through on it.從來都是這樣認為：
人生中，有大大小小的選擇。
人生的意義和學問，就是面對自己那些，負責地作出一個又一個的選擇，承擔及執行每個決定。

be content, and you will find joy.
discontent brings suffering.
those who recognise how much they still have to learn are blessed.知足常樂。不知足常苦。
知不足者，有福。

i am neither a priest nor a monk, but i share my teachings in my own way.
strictly speaking, i am not engaged in creative education. i am engaged in life education.我不是神父或僧人，但我用自己的方法傳道。
嚴格來說，我不是在做創意教育，我是在做生命教育。

through my travels and work, shaped by both luck and the relationships i have built along the way,
i have always believed that travelling ten thousand miles teaches you more than reading ten thousand books.
for people today, i would add one more thing:
clicking through ten thousand web pages is no substitute for stepping outside and travelling ten thousand miles.從運氣和工作關係的外訪經驗，我從來都相信，讀萬卷書不如行萬里路。
對現代人要加一句：click 一萬個 page，不如走出去行萬里路。

every day is a day for travelling.
travel is setting out.
travel is experiencing.
travel is exploring.
travel is embracing the unknown.
travel is the cape of good hope.
travel is mong kok and north point.
travel is the ends of the earth.
travel is the corner just downstairs from home.
sometimes, travel is planned for a particular day.
sometimes, any ordinary day can be a day for travelling.日日旅行日。
旅行是上路 旅行是體驗
旅行是探索 旅行是未知
旅行是好望角 旅行是旺角北角 旅行是天涯海角
旅行是屋企樓下轉角
旅行有時擇日定日
旅行可以平常日日

creativity should never be dismissed with a simple “no”.
creativity should ask: “why not?”創作應是不可說不
創作應是：為什麼「不」

nothing is absolute.
everything is relative.
what you see as nowhere,
i see as now here.
life depends on how you choose to see it.凡事沒有絕對
凡事講求相對
你看的 NOWHERE
我看見 NOW HERE
生命 在乎你怎樣看

when two people walk together, there may be disagreements.
when three or more walk together, there may be politics.
whenever we work with others, things may inevitably be left unfinished.
but our own destiny remains in our own hands.
life, i believe, should never be left unfinished.兩人走在一起，可有紛爭，
三人或以上走在一起，可有政治，
與他人共事，當然會有爛尾之可能，
但一己命運在一己之手，
人生，我想不應該爛尾吧！

do not take it to heart on the surface, but keep it in your heart.不放在眼內
但放在心裡

remember this:
you belong to this society. society is made up of countless individuals — people like you and me.
when you judge or criticise society, remember that you are part of it.
you share in its responsibility, and you have a role to play in shaping it.
if you have the ability and the responsibility, then take ownership. change the things you believe need to be changed.請記住，
你是屬於這個社會，社會是從很多個個體，很多個你或者我所組成的。
你做出的判斷、批判這個社會，這裡面是你有份的，你是有責任和參與的 ......
你有責任同能力，就要承擔，要改變你覺得需要改變的東西。

five things to do in life
- what you can do.
- what you love to do, enjoy doing, or are passionate about.
- what you ought to do.
- what needs to be done, or what others need you to do.
- what you are already doing.人生五做：
能做
愛做 / 喜歡做 / 鍾意做
應做
需要做 / 需要你做
附註 / 正在做

humanity.
worry.
excellence.人。
憂。
則優。(a play on the chinese words 人、憂、則優 — suggesting that through human concerns and reflection,
we may grow into something better.)

walk ahead of yourself.走在自己前面。

technology and information have expanded humanity’s reach and speed, but not its depth.
if anything, we seem to be growing increasingly shallow.科技資訊帶來人類寬度和速度，不是深度，反之然，愈來愈淺。

the course of life is a series of countless choices.
do not rely too heavily on your mind.
listen carefully to what your heart is telling you.
act with your heart.人生的進程就是無數的抉擇，
不要過於用腦。
清楚聽到自己心裏說話，
用心行事。

time
the past, relative to the future.
the key lies in the present moment.時間
過去相對未來
關鍵是當下

time: slow versus fast. neither wins nor loses.
moving forward is about speed, about exploration.
looking back is about slowing down, about finding inspiration.時間 慢相對快 沒勝負
向前走 是快 是探索
往後看 是慢 是靈感所在
