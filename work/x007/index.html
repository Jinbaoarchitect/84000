being hong kong就係香港
2018 mixed media / book跨媒體 / 書籍
being hong kong is a quarterly magazine that explores and tells local stories of hong kong, and at the same time re-affirms the power of text and writing. from conception, text review, image editing, typesetting, paper testing to printing, the publishing team experiments on creative combinations of content, visual effect and texture, so as to create unique reading experience to readers. the “being” media team is comprised of dedicated members from media, publishing, design creative, culture, art and marketing fields. all born and raised in hong kong, they join hands to discover stories of their hometown and its people. through different formats and channels, our love and hope towards the city truly revealed.「就係媒體」由一群來自傳媒、出版、設計創意、文化、藝術及市場推廣各個界別的工作者組成。通過各種形式與想像，說香港與香港人的故事。 一路走過來，在香港出生、成長，我們經歷了這個地方的繁榮，歲月令我們更清楚自己的信念。 我們仍然對人對生活充滿好奇，我們對香港仍充滿期盼和希望。通過Being HK「就係香港」季刊紙本為核心，並輔以網絡平台及實體活動，發掘未完的香港故事。
