it is said that “a picture tells more than a thousand words”. if a picture can speak more than a thousand words, then, what can be offered by a picture with just “a few words”? words are used to tell different stories by respective interpretation, being fermented and polished along with time, the plot becomes more vivid and touching. words can be compelling, but sometimes be taken apart. ideas can be put into words, but sometimes you have to read between the lines. at the crossroad, reminding me of the lyrics of “midnight whisper” by cantopop singer sam hui: babbling nonsense, more typos. however, we both know what's in our hearts. knowing but not knowing, conversing with myself ruthlessly. (a tribute to the heroes and unsung heroes written in the city of hong kong in the past forty years, granting me unlimited imagination and inspiration).人家説 a picture tells more than a thousand words 。 如果一幅照片能説多過千言萬語， 那麼，一張有「三言兩語」的照片，可能給我們什麼... 文字落在不同人的手中，訴説不同的故事心聲， 與時間磨合發酵，情節愈發立體、動人。 有時字字鏗鏘，有時説文會被解字。 有時白紙黑字，有時唯有字裡行間。 十字街頭，想起 sam hui 一句歌詞： 糊言亂語更多錯字，總之兩心知。 在知還不知當中，好好地、狠狠地，自言自語。 （ 向四十年來親身看見在香港我城書寫其中的英雄和無名英雄致意， 賦與我無限想像和靈感。） seeing words in hong kong / redwhiteblue //
