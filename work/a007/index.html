dance goes on冇照跳
2017 multimedia多媒體
a long-planned dance movie project, a dance experimentation project without a stage, audience or format boundaries, initiated by stanley wong (anothermountainman) and three of his good friends: mui cheuk-yin, xing liang and yuri ng, all accomplished choreographers. the three friends choreographed improvised “personal – exceptional” dances with the people, space and objects of hong kong, drawing on the essence of dance and the many issues surrounding our home, hong kong, the city we live in. their wish is for hong kong to stride forward and dance with its usual embracing and positive attitude; a wish for hong kong, “life goes on…”一個經長期醞釀的舞蹈電影計劃，由黃炳培（又一山人）連結三位舞蹈界的達人好友：梅卓燕、邢亮及伍宇烈，一起進行沒有舞台、沒有觀眾、不設形式界限的舞蹈實驗。 三人在電影中分別創作「非常」自身的舞蹈，重新探索香港的人、地、物，重新發現舞蹈的本質，以及自處今天香港這個城市的種種。期盼這城市能如昔以豁達包容，正面態度往前走，往前舞動；並寄語香港：life goes on…
