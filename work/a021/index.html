when is now? where do we go next? what did we do just now? work on a now? or do b first? thinking about b while working on a? live now. explicitly now. focus myself at now. the click of the :now clock is like the chime at a buddhist temple, calling my soul, my mind, back to now.現在是甚麼時候？ 跟住我們去那？ 剛才我們做了甚麼? 現在先做甲？還是先做乙？ 還是做甲途中，同時想乙？ 活在當下。 清晰在當下。 將自己凝聚在當下。 當:NOW 枱鐘咔地一聲， 就像禪堂引磬聲響起， 將心，神呼喚回來。 來到當下一己。
