a contemporary and educational exhibition of china’s forbidden city held at hong kong’s cultural centre. the cultural exhibition was designed to enhance the public’s interest and knowledge to chinese design culture on the topic of the forbidden city. the use of motion graphics recreated space in scale and video experience, engaging the audience, surprise and enlightens them by combining multimedia technologies and tradition culture. the exhibition has attracted audience from all over world which creatively showcases the distinct asian character and lifestyles.一個富現代和教育意味的大紫禁城展覽於香港交化中心舉行， 展覽旨在加強社會大眾對紫禁城設計交化的興趣和知識。 當中採納的動態圖像營造了大量空間和影片體驗，讓訪客參與其中。 在這揉合多媒體技術和文化的展覽中，為訪客帶來驚喜和啟迪。 展覽吸引了來自世界各地的訪客， 無疑是突顯亞洲風格和生活的絕佳渠道。
