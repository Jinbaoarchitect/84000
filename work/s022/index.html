can comics serve as a platform for collective thinking and collective creativity? can comics generate creativity? let’s try! dialoging with oneself can be criticized as a form of self indulgence, but i do believe creativity starts with self dialogue, and dialogue with others is the platform to initiate creativity and to generate cross-cultural collaboration. to have created this programme of creative platform, a project which reflects the creative spirit of hong kong, bringing out the creativity of various people, and projecting their collective creativity, but most important of all, is the bringing together of different creative spirits for collaboration. this figurine of tian tianis like a piece of blank paper, has drawn people closer together, to work and interact with each other under the same framework, without their realising it. they work together to explore the relationship between form and content, dream and vision, making the most of the spirit of tian tian xiang shang in which children take fanciful in their imagination and practise experimental learning.can comics serve as a platform for collective thinking and collective creativity?
