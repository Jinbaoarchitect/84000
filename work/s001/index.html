client客戶 /rosewood × hong kong palace museum香港瑰麗酒店 × 香港故宮文化博物館
unlike all mooncake packagings in market, this full moon story is imaginary and inspiring which created by ai with scientific datas. it blends transitional and contemporary chinese art and culture. designer has joined hands with local artist with the world’s first artificial intelligence ink painter a.i. gemini, to create a dialogue across time and space and reimagine the harvest moon through ink painting and state-of- the-art technology. the design approach certainly reflects the both rosewood and hong kong palaces museum’s spirit, prompting and embracing creative culture with spectrum from traditional to contemporary.與所有在市場出現之中秋節月餅禮盒設計方向截然不同... 這個副想像力之㘣月故事是由 AI 跟科學數據而創造出來的。 設計糅合嶄新，並傳承中國文化的精神。平面設計師跟本地視覺藝術家及他的 a.i. gemini ， 以中國傳統的水墨畫概念，融入古人對中秋滿月的想像及嫦娥四號完成世界首次月球背面軟着陸的相關數據。 利用由創新的人工智能所輔助的設計及跨越時空的對話，重新詮釋現代水墨畫的傳承。 這個設計的方向，正正反映香港瑰麗酒店和香港故宮文化博物館雙方的精神， 推廣和擁抱創意文化在傳統、傳承和未來的探索。
