client客戶 /art basel香港巴塞爾藝術展
during covid 19, hong kong city were almost stopped and people were isolated with health policy for most of daily routine… certainly, art was a meaningful drive to bring people together, again… a poster / advertisement campaign was captured the key people from art, cultural scenes including art lovers, cheerfully blending together, to celebrate the special art basel hong kong 2021 episode for a special moment of the city.在新冠疫情期間，香港幾乎陷入停滯，人們的日常生活大多受到防疫政策的限制… 藝術無疑是推動人們再次相聚的重要力量… 一系列海報/廣告宣傳活動捕捉了藝術界和文化界的關鍵人物， 包括藝術愛好者在內的各界人士歡聚一堂， 共同慶祝2021年香港巴塞爾藝術展這一特殊時刻，為這座城市增添獨特的色彩。
