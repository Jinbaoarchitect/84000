acknowledgments鳴謝

looking back, i realise that none of the work was ever made alone.
behind every idea, every image and every project were people
who shared their time, trust, patience and passion with me.
my wife, my team, the artists, colleagues, clients and friends
i have worked with over the years —
each of you has been part of the journey in your own way.
there were long nights, difficult moments, unexpected turns,
good conversations and plenty of laughter.
some things worked, some did not. but all of them became part of the story.
in the end, perhaps the work is not the most important thing we leave behind.
it is the people we meet,
the things we make together, and the memories we carry with us.
thank you for being part of mine.

special thanks //
jessie law

回望過去，我發現沒有一件作品是獨自完成的。
每一個意念、每一幅影像、每一個項目背後，
都有人與我分享他們的時間、信任、耐性和熱情。
我的太太、我的團隊、藝術家、同事、客戶和朋友，
多年來與我共事的每一位——
你們都以各自的方式，成為這段旅程的一部分。
有過漫漫長夜、艱難時刻、意料之外的轉折，
也有過暢快的對話和許多歡笑。
有些事成了，有些沒有；但它們都成為了故事的一部分。
到頭來，或許作品並不是我們留下的最重要的東西。
重要的是我們遇上的人、
一起創造的事，以及我們帶着走的回憶。
感謝你成為我回憶的一部分。

特別鳴謝 //
jessie law
